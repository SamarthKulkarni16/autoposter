## IMPORTANT TRAP I HIT (don't repeat)
Scanning raw body.inner_text for "can't be uploaded" is a false-positive trap
(a reject toast persists as hidden DOM text). Base registration/success on the
VISIBLE actionable state (placeholder gone in live composer / Post enabled),
not raw body substring scans.
